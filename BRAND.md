# Wick Wax & Relax — Brand DNA

> Source of every unique decision on this site. Extracted from the live store's
> own material (homepage copy, product records in `scripts/seed-showcase.sql`,
> category set, packaging/compliance copy, the existing "The Ritual" design
> system in `frontend/src/theme.js`). Nothing here is invented — where a fact
> is not on record it is marked `[CONFIRM]`.

## 1. Name and story

- **Name:** *Wick Wax & Relax*. A command, not a description: wick the wax, then
  relax. The name is the product promise in three words.
- **What it is:** hand-poured soy candles, wax melts, bath bombs and reed
  diffusers. Small batches. Sold direct.
- **Founded / maker:** `[CONFIRM: founder name, year founded, solo or
  family-run]`. The site's own voice is first-person plural and hands-on
  ("we pour", "our studio"), never corporate.
- **The origin story in one line:** *made to slow the room down.*

## 2. Place

- **Sussex, UK.** Every product line says "Made in the UK" / "Poured in Sussex
  by hand"; the footer repeats it as a trust line.
- Landscape cue: not coastal, not urban — **rural English workshop**. The
  imagery is interiors and hands, not fields.
- The customer buys for the **room**, not for the object: the mood tiles
  ("To Unwind", "To Wake", "To Celebrate", "To Reset") are the site's own
  positioning and the thing a competitor's grid cannot copy.

## 4. Existing brand assets (preserved — do not redesign)

- **Palette:** deep plum `#3D2B3D` (dark sections, CTAs), plum-deep `#2A1D2A`
  (ink on plum), paper `#FDFCFA`, sand `#F4EFE7`, ink `#1C1A1F`, clay
  `#C9846B` (urgency/eyebrow accents), lavender `#C8B6DB` (secondary accent),
  gold `#E6C88A` (the flame accent on dark surfaces).
- **Type:** Playfair Display (display serif, weights 400–700) + Montserrat
  (UI/body, 300–700), both self-hosted woff2 via `next/font/local`.
- **Monogram:** circular plum "W" roundel in the header.
- **Motif:** the warm brand grade over every photograph (plum → amber
  soft-light) plus a film-grain layer — this is what makes unrelated stock
  photography read as one art-directed campaign.

## 5. Artefacts of the trade

- The **CLP label** (EU Reg. 1223/2009): hazard pictograms, signal word,
  precautionary statements, allergens declared. Almost nobody at this price
  band can say it, so it doubles as a differentiator.
- The **batch**: twelve candles a batch, never more.
- The **cure**: fourteen days before a candle may leave the studio.

## 7. The difference (one thing competitors do not do)

**Sell by occasion, not by product type** — and back the premium with
process, not adjectives: the batch size and the fourteen-day cure, stated in
plain numbers, plus CLP/allergen transparency on every scent.

## 8. Positioning modifiers applied

- **Price tier:** premium (£11.50–£20 per unit) → more space, fewer colours,
  larger imagery, slower motion.
- **Audience:** UK home-fragrance buyers, mostly on mobile, shopping in the
  evening on a phone → **mobile is the primary surface**, not a fallback.
- **Urgency:** considered purchase → storytelling, proof, depth of content.
- **Buyer:** consumers → emotion, imagery, personality; price visible without
  a click (the site's own "Amazon's conversion spine").

## 9. Safe zone (never to be removed)

- Price and RRP visible; "Save %" is honest arithmetic on the RRP.
- Free UK delivery over £25 · 30-day returns · Plastic-free packaging ·
  Hand-poured in the UK.
- CLP labelling and allergen declaration (compliance, not marketing).
- No invented reviews, ratings, bestseller claims or stock urgency.
- Real product names, notes and photography — never lorem ipsum.

## 10. Avoid

- Pastel-gradient "wellness" branding; generic apothecary serif kits.
- Emoji as icons. Stock-photo hands. Beeswax-clipart. Lavender-field clichés.
- Faux-testimonial carousels and invented counts.
- Anything that makes the site look like a theme demo: centred hero + two
  buttons, three identical rounded cards, a row of equal product cards.
